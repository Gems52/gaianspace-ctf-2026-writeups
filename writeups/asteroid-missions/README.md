# Asteroid missions

A prospecting, recovery and landing campaign around the near-Earth asteroid **2041 QM23** and a companion moonlet: spotting the metal-rich body, recovering it with a short-arc orbit fit, landing on its metal patch, sampling the moonlet and synchronising the lander and mothership clocks.
